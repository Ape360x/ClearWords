# ClearWords demo video script (target 2:30, hard limit 3:00)

Record the screen with your voice over it. Run `npm start` first and open http://localhost:3000. Use a live API key for the recording if you have one, otherwise demo mode is fine.

**0:00 – 0:20 Hook**
Say: "Imagine being told you have cancer, handed a page of medical English, and walking out not understanding it. That's what happens to many patients in Nigeria. ClearWords fixes that."
Show: the home page.

**0:20 – 0:50 Problem + user**
Say: "ClearWords is for patients and caregivers. You paste text from a pathology report, scan result or clinic note, choose the language you actually speak at home, and get a plain-language explanation."
Show: click "Use a sample report", point at the dense text.

**0:50 – 1:40 Core demo**
1. Pick English, click "Explain it to me". Read the simple summary, scroll the glossary.
2. Switch to Pidgin and run again. Say: "Same report, now in Nigerian Pidgin."
3. Show the questions to ask your doctor. Say: "It also builds questions for the next appointment."

**1:40 – 2:05 Accessibility**
Click "Read aloud". Say: "Not everyone is comfortable reading, so it reads results aloud. No login, nothing is stored, and the page is light enough for slow networks."
Show: the language-quality warning on Yoruba/Igbo/Hausa. Say: "When our language quality is lower, we say so honestly."

**2:05 – 2:30 Safety + close**
Say: "ClearWords never diagnoses, never predicts outcomes, never recommends treatment. It explains the words and sends people back to their care team, better prepared. Thank you."
Show: the "Not medical advice" notice and the "when to get help" box.

Tips: do one full practice run first, keep the cursor still while talking, and export under 3:00.
